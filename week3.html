Chapter 1
How AI Sees Pictures
Pixels, colours, and how computers read images
Hey Marty! 👁️ When you look at a photo of a cat, you instantly see a fluffy animal. But a computer just sees a giant grid of tiny coloured squares called PIXELS. Each pixel is just a number! Let's find out how computers turn those millions of numbers into understanding what's in a picture.
What a computer actually sees 🔢
👤
What YOU see
A cute orange cat sitting on a blue mat. Instantly! Your brain figures it all out in a split second using everything you've ever learned about cats.
💻
What AI sees
255,128,0,255,127,1,254,129... Millions of numbers! Each one represents the brightness of Red, Green, and Blue in one tiny pixel.
🔤 Key words
Pixel
The tiniest dot in a digital image — like one square in a mosaic
Resolution
How many pixels an image has — more pixels = sharper image
RGB
Red, Green, Blue — the three colour values that mix to make every colour on a screen
📸
A photo on your iPad might have 12 MEGAPIXELS — that's 12 million pixels! Each pixel has 3 numbers (R, G, B), so the AI processes 36 million numbers just to "see" ONE photo. And it does this in milliseconds!
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q1: What is a pixel?
🔬 Activity Time!
See the Pixels!
1
Open any photo on your iPad.
2
Use two fingers to zoom in as far as you can go.
3
Can you see the tiny squares? Those are pixels! Each one is a single colour.
4
Now zoom out — your brain magically blends them all into a picture. AI has to do that with maths!
💡 Try a photo with a bright coloured area — like a rainbow or a sunset. Zoomed in, you can really see the pixel grid clearly!

Chapter 2
Face Recognition
How your iPad knows it's YOU
Every time you pick up your iPad and it unlocks — that's AI! Face ID uses a tiny dot projector that shoots 30,000 invisible dots at your face, creates a 3D map, and checks it against the stored map of your face. All in less than a second. Let's explore how this amazing technology works!
📡
Step 1: Scan
30,000 tiny infrared dots are projected onto your face, creating a detailed 3D depth map.
🗺️
Step 2: Map
The depth map captures the unique shape of YOUR face — distance between your eyes, nose depth, jaw shape.
🔢
Step 3: Math
Your face is converted to a mathematical pattern — a unique "face signature" stored securely.
✅
Step 4: Match
AI compares today's face signature to the stored one. Close enough? Unlock! Doesn't match? Stay locked.
🔒
PRIVACY FACT: Your face data never leaves your device! It's stored in a special secure chip called the Secure Enclave — not in the cloud, not on Apple's servers. Even Apple can't see your face map. Pretty cool security design!
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q1: How does Face ID actually recognise your face?
📸 Activity Time!
Test Face ID!
1
Try to unlock your iPad with your face in different conditions — normal lighting, dim light, wearing sunglasses.
2
What still works? What confuses it?
3
Ask a family member to try unlocking it. Does it work for them?
4
Why do you think it works in the dark? (Hint: infrared light is invisible to humans but not to sensors!)
💡 Infrared light works in the dark — that's why Face ID works even when the room is pitch black. This is a great "wow" moment for kids!

Chapter 3
AI & Videos
How AI understands moving pictures
A video is just thousands of photos played really fast — typically 24 to 60 photos (frames) every single second! To understand a video, AI has to process each frame AND understand how things move and change over time. That's MUCH harder than just looking at one picture!
Video = many photos
30 frames per second × 60 seconds = 1,800 images in just one minute of video!
AI watches for changes
It notices what moved, what appeared, what disappeared — not just what's there.
Action recognition
AI can tell if someone is running, dancing, falling — just from analysing movement patterns.
Real uses today
Security cameras, self-driving cars, sports analysis, film special effects!
🎬
MOVIE MAGIC: When you watch a superhero movie and a character flies through a city, AI is doing a LOT of work! It tracks real actors, replaces backgrounds, adds digital characters, and makes everything look real — all by analysing thousands of video frames!
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q1: A 1-minute video at 30 frames per second has how many frames for AI to analyse?
🎥 Activity Time!
Slow-Mo Video Exploration!
1
Open the Camera app on your iPad and switch to Slow-Mo mode.
2
Record something fast — a ball bouncing, water splashing, or clapping hands.
3
Play it back and watch in slow motion. You can now see what AI sees — individual moments!
4
This is how AI "breaks down" motion to understand what's happening frame by frame.
💡 Slow motion captures 240 frames per second — 8x more than normal video. This is literally what AI processing feels like — seeing every tiny moment in detail!

Chapter 4
Talking to AI
How Siri hears and understands you
When you say "Hey Siri!" your voice travels as invisible waves through the air, gets captured by a tiny microphone, turned into numbers, and sent to an AI that figures out what you said AND what you meant. All in under a second. Let's break down this incredible journey!
🌊
Sound waves
Your voice creates invisible pressure waves in the air — like ripples on a pond. The microphone detects these vibrations.
🔢
Digitising
The waves become numbers — thousands of measurements per second. This is called "sampling."
🗣️
Speech recognition
AI matches the sound patterns to words it learned from millions of hours of human speech recordings.
🧠
Understanding
A second AI figures out what the words MEAN — "set a timer for 5 minutes" vs "what's the weather like today?"
🌍
AI assistants like Siri can now understand over 20 different languages! To learn each language, the AI studied thousands of hours of speech from native speakers. Learning English alone required listening to more hours of speech than a human could hear in several lifetimes!
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q1: When you speak to Siri, what does the microphone convert your voice into first?
🎙️ Activity Time!
Test Siri with Funny Questions!
1
Ask Siri some tricky questions — see if it understands accents, whispers, or funny voices!
2
Try whispering — does it still work?
3
Try saying the same thing in different ways. Does Siri always understand?
4
When does it get confused? That tells you where AI speech recognition still struggles!
💡 Try: "Set a timer for two minutes" then "Set a timer for 2 minutes" — does it handle both? Testing AI's limits is a great way to understand how it works!

Chapter 5
AI That Reads Text
How AI understands words and sentences
Reading is HARD — even for humans! Think about how many years it took you to learn to read. AI had to learn the same thing — but it did it by reading billions of books, websites, and articles. Now it can understand sentences, answer questions, and even translate languages. Let's see how!
🔤 Key words
NLP
Natural Language Processing — the science of teaching AI to understand human language
Token
A chunk of text (usually a word or part of a word) that AI processes one piece at a time
Context
Understanding meaning based on surrounding words — "bat" means different things in baseball vs. nature!
🔍
Search engines
Understand what you're really looking for
🌍
Translators
Convert text between 100+ languages instantly
📧
Email filters
Read emails to spot spam and sort your inbox
🔮
AUTOCOMPLETE MAGIC: When you type on your iPad, it suggests the next word. That's AI reading what you've written and predicting what comes next! It learned this by studying billions of sentences and noticing which words usually follow other words. It's like a very smart "fill in the blank" game!
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q1: What does "context" mean for AI language understanding?
🔮 Activity Time!
Autocomplete Guessing Game!
1
Open Notes or Messages on your iPad.
2
Start typing a sentence slowly. Before you type each word, PREDICT what the autocomplete will suggest.
3
Were you right? Keep tapping the first autocomplete suggestion and see what sentence the AI "writes."
4
The AI sentence might not make sense — why? Because it only predicts one word at a time without planning the whole sentence!
💡 This reveals a real limitation of simple AI — it's very good at "what word usually comes next" but can lose track of the overall meaning. Advanced AI like ChatGPT is trained to plan whole responses, not just one word at a time.

Chapter 6
AI That Writes
Stories, essays, and where AI creativity comes from
AI can now write stories, poems, essays, jokes, and even code! Tools like ChatGPT have read so much human writing that they can produce text that sounds just like a person wrote it. But does AI really understand what it's writing? Or is it just a very sophisticated "fill in the blank" machine? Let's explore!
✅
What AI writing is good at
  • Following a structure (beginning, middle, end)
  • Writing in different styles (formal, funny, poetic)
  • Generating ideas quickly
  • Summarising long texts
⚠️
What AI writing struggles with
  • Always being 100% accurate and truthful
  • Personal experiences it has never had
  • Truly original ideas (everything is based on training data)
  • Knowing when it's wrong
🤖
YOUR voice matters! AI writing is trained on human writing — which means YOUR creativity, YOUR stories, and YOUR unique way of seeing the world are things AI can never replace. AI is a tool that helps you write MORE — but the truly original spark always starts with a human brain like yours!
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q1: When AI writes a story, where does its "creativity" come from?
✍️ Activity Time!
Co-Write a Story with AI!
1
Marty writes the opening sentence of a story — make it exciting!
2
Ask AI (Dad types it into ChatGPT or similar) to continue the story for 2 sentences.
3
Marty adds 2 more sentences, then AI adds 2, back and forth.
4
Read the finished story! Who came up with the most creative parts — Marty or the AI?
💡 This is a beautiful way to show AI as a creative partner, not a replacement. Marty's opening sentence will likely be more imaginative than AI's — because it comes from HIS unique experiences!

Chapter 7
AI That Makes Art
How AI generates images and what it means for artists
Tools like DALL-E and Midjourney can turn your words into pictures! Type "a robot reading a book on the moon" and within seconds, AI creates an image that's never existed before. How is this possible? And is it really "art" if a machine made it? Let's dive in!
🔤 New words
Prompt
The description you type to tell AI what image to create
Diffusion
The process AI uses — it starts with random noise and slowly "clears" it into an image
Generative AI
AI that creates NEW content — images, text, music, video
🖌️
HOW IT WORKS: AI image generators learned from hundreds of millions of images and their descriptions. When you type "sunset over mountains," it recalls all the visual patterns associated with those words and combines them. It's like a super-powered collage artist who has seen every painting ever made!
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q1: What is a "prompt" when using AI art tools?
🎨 Activity Time!
Create AI Art Together!
1
Marty: think of the most creative, detailed image you can imagine. Write it down!
2
Dad: type Marty's description into an AI art tool (try Adobe Firefly at firefly.adobe.com — free and kid-safe).
3
Look at what comes out! Is it what Marty imagined? What's different?
4
Try tweaking the prompt — add words like "watercolour style" or "bright colours" and see how it changes!
💡 Adobe Firefly is specifically designed to be ethical — it only trained on licensed images. It's also free to try without signing up. Perfect for kids!

Chapter 8
AI That Makes Music
How AI composes and why music has patterns
Music is pure pattern! Rhythms, melodies, harmonies — they're all mathematical patterns that follow rules. And if there's one thing AI is amazing at, it's finding and reproducing patterns! Today AI can compose entire songs, create background music, and even match music to your mood. Let's explore!
🎼
Music = patterns
A beat repeats every few seconds. Melodies follow scales. Chords follow progressions. AI learns all these rules from analysing millions of songs.
🤖
AI composing
AI can generate music in any style — classical, pop, jazz. It picks notes, rhythms, and instruments based on patterns it learned from human composers.
🎸
REAL EXAMPLE: Spotify uses AI to create personalised playlists for every single listener. It analyses what songs you've played, when you skipped, how long you listened, and even what time of day — then creates a playlist it predicts you'll love. Over 600 million people use it every day!
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q1: Why is music especially easy for AI to work with?
🎵 Activity Time!
Explore Chrome Music Lab!
1
Go to musiclab.chromeexperiments.com on your iPad (no app needed, just Safari!).
2
Try the "Song Maker" — create a melody by tapping the grid.
3
Notice how the coloured squares create a visual pattern that matches the musical pattern!
4
AI sees music EXACTLY like this — as a grid of patterns. You're seeing through AI's eyes!
💡 Chrome Music Lab is made by Google and is completely free, kid-friendly, and works in Safari on iPad. The visual representation of music as a grid is literally how AI processes audio — this activity makes that concept concrete!

Chapter 9
Senses AI Doesn't Have (Yet!)
Touch, smell, taste — still a huge challenge
AI has gotten amazing at seeing, hearing, and reading. But what about your other senses? Imagine trying to teach a computer what "warm" feels like, or what the smell of rain is like, or how pineapple on pizza tastes! These are things AI still struggles HUGELY with. Your senses give you superpowers AI doesn't have!
✅
AI is great at
  • Seeing (computer vision)
  • Hearing (speech recognition)
  • Reading (natural language)
  • Detecting some physical sensors
😓
AI struggles with
  • Touch — feeling texture, temperature, pain
  • Smell — identifying and describing scents
  • Taste — understanding flavour combinations
  • Emotional gut feelings and intuition
👃
SCIENTISTS ARE WORKING ON IT: Researchers have built "electronic noses" that can detect certain smells using chemical sensors. They're used to detect gas leaks, check food freshness, and even spot some diseases! But describing WHAT a smell is like, the way you can, is still incredibly hard for machines.
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q1: Which of these can current AI do best?
👃 Activity Time!
Blindfold Smell Test — Can AI Do This?
1
Collect 5 things with strong scents — orange peel, coffee, soap, cinnamon, vanilla extract.
2
Marty closes his eyes. Dad holds each one under his nose. Can Marty identify them all?
3
Marty will probably get most right! Now think: how would you teach a robot to do this?
4
Could you describe the smell in enough words for a robot to learn it? This is exactly why smell AI is so hard!
💡 Smell is deeply connected to memory and emotion in humans — that's partly why it's so hard to separate from experience and teach to a machine. Great conversation about what makes human senses special!
