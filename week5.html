Chapter 1
AI Doctors
Finding diseases in X-rays and scans
When a doctor needs to look at thousands of X-rays to find a tiny tumour, it can take hours and even the best doctors can miss something. AI can analyse those same X-rays in seconds — and is sometimes MORE accurate than human doctors! Let's explore how AI is saving lives in medicine.
How AI reads X-rays
🖼️ Gets the X-ray
🔢 Converts to numbers
🧠 Compares to millions
🔍 Spots unusual patterns
⚠️ Flags to doctor
💡
AI can spot tiny tumours just 1mm in size — smaller than the tip of a pencil! In some studies, AI detected certain cancers more accurately than experienced radiologists. It doesn't replace doctors — it gives them a brilliant second opinion.
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q: What is one important thing you learned in this chapter?
🎯 Activity Time!
role-play activity
1
Use a toy stethoscope and pretend to examine stuffed animals.

3
Now imagine: what information would you give an AI to help it diagnose patients?
4
Think about what data a medical AI would need: symptoms, test results, patient history.
💡 This connects diagnosis to data collection — the key insight is that AI doctors need the same information human doctors collect, just structured as data!

Chapter 2
AI Scientists
Discovering medicines and new materials
Developing a new medicine the traditional way takes 10-15 years and costs billions of dollars. AI is changing this dramatically! It can analyse millions of possible chemical combinations and predict which ones might work as medicines — narrowing down the search from millions of options to just a handful to test.
Drug discovery with AI
🧪 Start: millions of molecules
🤖 AI screens them
✅ Finds promising candidates
🔬 Scientists test top ones
💊 New medicine!
💡
During COVID-19, AI helped scientists understand the virus structure and identify potential vaccine targets in DAYS — work that would normally take months or years. AI-assisted drug discovery could be one of the biggest life-saving technologies of your lifetime!
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q: What is one important thing you learned in this chapter?
🎯 Activity Time!
hands-on science
1
Mix bicarbonate of soda and vinegar — observe the reaction!
2
Now think: a scientist might mix MILLIONS of different chemicals to find a new medicine.
3
AI can 'virtually mix' millions of combinations in seconds to predict what will work.
4
How many experiments would a human need to do that manually?
💡 The AI drug discovery process is literally millions of virtual experiments per second. Compared to one fizzing reaction at a time, the scale difference is mind-boggling for kids!

Chapter 3
AI & Climate
Predicting weather and fighting climate change
Climate change is one of the biggest challenges your generation will face — and AI is one of the most powerful tools available to help! From predicting extreme weather to designing better solar panels, AI is working hard to protect our planet.
Ways AI fights climate change
🌪️ Predicts storms days early
☀️ Optimises solar & wind power
🚗 Makes cars more fuel-efficient
🌳 Tracks deforestation via satellite
🌡️ Models future climate scenarios
💡
Google's DeepMind AI improved the accuracy of 10-day weather forecasts dramatically. Better forecasts mean farmers can plan better, power companies can prepare, and people can get out of the way of dangerous storms. AI could save thousands of lives through better weather prediction alone!
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q: What is one important thing you learned in this chapter?
🎯 Activity Time!
prediction activity
1
Look outside and make a weather prediction for tomorrow.
2
Write down your prediction with your reasoning.
3
Check the real forecast — how did you do?
4
Now look up how meteorologists collect data: weather balloons, satellites, ocean buoys. AI processes all of this!
💡 Weather forecasting requires processing data from thousands of sensors worldwide simultaneously — exactly the type of large-scale pattern recognition that AI excels at!

Chapter 4
AI Farmers
Smart farming for more food with less waste
Did you know that about a third of all food grown in the world is wasted? And farming uses more water than any other human activity? AI is helping farmers grow MORE food using LESS water, fewer chemicals, and less land. This is incredibly important as our planet's population keeps growing!
Smart farming AI in action
🛸
Drone cameras scan crops
🤖
AI spots diseases early
💧
Smart irrigation delivers exact water needed
🌱
Predicts best planting times
📊
Monitors soil health continuously
💡
Some AI-powered farms can grow crops using 95% less water than traditional farms! Computer vision cameras scan fields looking for signs of disease or pests on individual plants — catching problems before they spread. One drone can monitor what would take a farmer days to walk.
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q: What is one important thing you learned in this chapter?
🎯 Activity Time!
gardening activity
1
Plant a seed in a small pot (beans germinate fast!).
2
Each day, measure its growth and record it in a notebook — you're collecting data!
3
Think: what data would a smart farming AI collect? (soil moisture, temperature, sunlight hours, height)
4
Design your own 'smart garden sensor' on paper — what would it measure?
💡 Growing something and measuring it daily is a beautiful way to understand agricultural data collection. The key insight: a smart farm AI just collects and analyses the same information a great farmer already pays attention to!

Chapter 5
AI in Space
Mars rovers and telescope image analysis
Space is HUGE — the nearest star to our Sun is so far away that light takes 4 years to travel there. There's simply too much of space for humans to explore manually. AI is helping us explore more of the universe than ever before — from analysing telescope images to navigating Mars rovers!
AI exploring space
🔭
James Webb Telescope: AI analyses billions of star images
🤖
Mars rovers: AI navigates terrain autonomously
⭐
Planet detection: AI finds planets around distant stars
🌌
Dark matter: AI maps invisible matter in the universe
💡
The James Webb Space Telescope sends back so much data that it would be impossible for humans to analyse it all manually. AI scans the images automatically, flagging interesting objects for scientists to examine. It's already discovered thousands of new galaxies that were previously unknown!
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q: What is one important thing you learned in this chapter?
🎯 Activity Time!
engineering activity
1
Draw your own Mars rover on paper.
2
Think about: what sensors would it have? What AI decisions would it need to make?
3
Remember: signals from Mars take 20 minutes to reach Earth! The rover must think for itself.
4
What challenges would your AI rover face on Mars?
💡 The communication delay is a crucial design constraint — any Mars rover AI must be truly autonomous because remote control is impossible in real-time. This naturally introduces the concept of autonomous AI decision-making!

Chapter 6
AI in the Ocean
Finding sea creatures and mapping the deep
Over 80% of Earth's oceans are still unexplored! The deep ocean is as mysterious to us as outer space. AI is helping oceanographers explore and understand this vast unknown world — from identifying whale songs to mapping underwater volcanoes and tracking plastic pollution.
Ocean AI missions
🎵
Listens to whale songs and translates their patterns
🐠
Identifies fish species from underwater camera footage
🗺️
Maps the ocean floor using sonar data
🌊
Tracks ocean plastic and pollution spread
🦑
Discovers new species in deep sea footage
💡
Researchers used AI to analyse thousands of hours of underwater recordings and discovered that whale communication has complex structure — almost like a language! AI found patterns in whale clicks that human researchers had studied for decades without noticing. The ocean is full of secrets AI is helping to unlock.
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q: What is one important thing you learned in this chapter?
🎯 Activity Time!
creative science activity
1
Research one deep sea creature — anglerfish, giant squid, or barreleye fish.
2
Draw the creature in its environment.
3
Imagine you're designing an AI to identify it in deep sea footage. What features would the AI look for?
4
What challenges would there be — darkness, strange shapes, never-seen-before creatures?
💡 Deep sea AI faces unique challenges: no light for vision, huge pressure, and creatures that look nothing like surface animals. This highlights that AI has to be specially trained for its environment!

Chapter 7
AI & Education
Personalised learning and tutoring bots
Every student learns differently — some learn quickly through reading, others need to hear things explained, others learn best by doing. Traditional classrooms struggle to teach 30 students all at once in the perfect way for each person. AI tutors could change this completely!
What AI tutors can do
📊
Identifies exactly where each student is struggling
🎯
Adjusts difficulty to always be perfectly challenging
⏱️
Gives instant feedback — no waiting for the teacher
💪
Provides extra practice on weak areas
📈
Shows parents and teachers detailed progress data
💡
Duolingo — the language learning app — uses AI to personalise every lesson. It knows exactly which words you keep forgetting and serves them up right before you're about to forget them (a technique called spaced repetition). Users learn languages 2-3× faster than traditional classes!
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q: What is one important thing you learned in this chapter?
🎯 Activity Time!
creative design activity
1
On paper, design an AI tutor for YOUR perfect learning experience.
2
What subject does it teach? How does it explain things? What's it called?
3
What would make a perfect lesson? What would make a terrible one?
4
What data would your AI tutor need to track to get better at teaching you?
💡 This activity develops metacognition — thinking about HOW you learn — which is one of the most valuable skills for lifelong learning. Kids who understand their own learning style learn faster!

Chapter 8
AI & Accessibility
Helping people who are blind, deaf, or have disabilities
One of the most beautiful uses of AI is helping people who experience the world differently. AI is giving blind people the ability to 'see,' helping deaf people access spoken conversations, and giving people with limited mobility new ways to communicate and control technology.
AI making the world more accessible
👁️
Seeing AI: describes photos and scenes aloud for blind users
👂
Live captions: transcribes speech to text in real time for deaf users
🗣️
Eye-tracking: lets people control computers using just their eyes
✋
Sign language AI: translates sign language to text and back
🧠
Brain-computer interfaces: let paralysed people type with thoughts
💡
Microsoft's Seeing AI app narrates the world for blind users — it reads text aloud, describes photos, identifies faces and emotions, and even reads product barcodes. One blind user said it was like having a friend always at their side who could describe everything they couldn't see. AI as kindness!
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q: What is one important thing you learned in this chapter?
🎯 Activity Time!
empathy activity
1
Ask Dad to find the Accessibility settings on your iPad.
2
Turn on VoiceOver (the screen reader) and try navigating without looking at the screen.
3
Try for just 2 minutes — how frustrating is it?
4
Now imagine how important this technology is for someone who is completely blind. Turn it back off and appreciate your sight!
💡 This is a powerful empathy exercise. Experiencing assistive technology firsthand — even briefly — creates lasting appreciation for how AI serves people with disabilities. Very few kids get this perspective!

Chapter 9
AI Can Be Wrong!
Bias, errors, and why AI isn't always right
We've spent the whole week learning about all the amazing things AI can do. But it's really important to understand that AI is NOT perfect — it makes mistakes, it can be biased, and sometimes it gets things spectacularly wrong. Being a smart AI user means knowing when to trust AI and when to question it!
Types of AI errors
🎭 Hallucination
AI confidently states things that are completely wrong
⚖️ Bias
Unfair outcomes because training data wasn't balanced
🌊 Distribution shift
AI works in training but fails on real-world data
🎯 Adversarial attacks
Tiny changes to input can fool AI completely
💡
AI language models sometimes 'hallucinate' — they make up facts with complete confidence! This happens because AI generates text based on patterns, not actual knowledge. Always verify important information from AI with reliable sources. Being a critical thinker is MORE important in the age of AI, not less!
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q: What is one important thing you learned in this chapter?
🎯 Activity Time!
critical thinking activity
1
Read this rule: 'People from City A are better at sport than people from City B.'
2
Is this fair? Why not? (Not everyone from one place is the same!)
3
If AI was trained on sports data that only covered certain cities, it might learn this wrong pattern.
4
Design a fairer way to collect sports data. What would you include?
💡 This activity introduces bias detection in a completely non-threatening, relatable context. The city sports example is arbitrary enough to feel obviously unfair while being structurally identical to real-world AI bias cases.
