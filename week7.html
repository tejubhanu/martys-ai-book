Chapter 1
Think Like an AI Builder
The mindset and steps every AI project needs
Welcome to the most exciting week yet, Marty! 🛠️ This week you're not just LEARNING about AI — you're BUILDING it! Before we write a single line of code or train a single model, let's get into the AI builder's mindset. Every great AI project starts with the same steps!
The 5 steps every AI project follows
1
🎯 Define the problem
What exactly do you want your AI to do? Be super specific!
2
📦 Collect your data
Get examples of what your AI needs to learn. Photos, words, sounds.
3
🏋️ Train the model
Show your AI the examples and let it learn the patterns.
4
🧪 Test and improve
Try it on new data. If it's wrong, fix the training and try again!
5
🚀 Deploy and share
Show the world what you built! Let people use your AI.
🏆
Every AI you've ever used — Siri, Google Search, ChatGPT, YouTube recommendations — went through exactly these 5 steps. You're about to join the same club as the world's top engineers. The only difference is scale!

Chapter 2
Scratch + AI Blocks
MIT's free tool for coding your first AI project
Scratch is a FREE coding tool made by MIT — the world's most famous tech university — specifically for kids. You snap together colourful blocks to write programs, no typing needed! And with an extension called ML4Kids, you can add real AI to your Scratch projects. Let's dive in!
🔤 Scratch words
Sprite
A character in Scratch — could be a cat, a rocket, a custom drawing!
Block
A piece of code you snap together — like a LEGO brick for programming
Script
A stack of blocks that runs when triggered (like pressing a key)
Extension
Extra blocks you can add to Scratch for special features like AI
✅
Scratch is great for
  • Learning to code by snapping blocks
  • Making games and stories
  • Adding AI using ML4Kids blocks
  • Sharing projects with the world
🌐
Where to find it
  • scratch.mit.edu — completely free!
  • Works in Safari on iPad
  • No download needed
  • Millions of projects to remix
🌍
Over 100 million people have used Scratch — including many professional programmers who learned to code on it as kids! The Scratch community is huge and friendly. You can see other kids' projects, remix them, and share your own. It's social media for young coders!
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q: What is a "sprite" in Scratch?
🐱 Activity Time!
Your First Scratch Program!
1
Open scratch.mit.edu in Safari on your iPad or on the family computer.
2
Click "Create" to start a new project. You'll see the orange cat sprite already there!
3
Drag out a "When green flag clicked" block, then add a "Say Hello for 2 seconds" block under it.
4
Click the green flag! Your cat says hello. Congratulations — you just coded your first program! Change the message to say YOUR name.
💡 Take a photo of Marty at the moment his first program runs. This is genuinely a milestone worth celebrating — it's the first step of a potentially lifelong skill. Frame it or print it!

Chapter 3
Build a Simple Chatbot
IF/THEN rules to create a conversation
A chatbot is a program that has a conversation with you. The very first chatbots (back in 1966!) used simple IF/THEN rules — "if the user says X, reply with Y." You already know all about IF/THEN from Week 4! Today you're going to build your own chatbot using exactly that idea.
How a rule-based chatbot works
User says:
"Hello"
Chatbot checks:
IF input contains "hello" THEN reply "Hi there! I'm MartyBot! What's your name?"
User says:
"My name is Sam"
Chatbot checks:
IF input contains "name is" THEN extract name and reply "Nice to meet you, Sam!"
🤖
ELIZA — the world's first chatbot, made at MIT in 1966 — fooled many people into thinking they were talking to a real therapist! It only had about 200 rules but was so convincing that people shared their deepest feelings with it. Rule-based AI can be surprisingly powerful!
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q: What does a rule-based chatbot do when it doesn't recognise what you said?
💬 Activity Time!
Write Your Own ChatBot Script!
1
On paper, design "MartyBot" — a chatbot with a personality! Give it a name, topic, and style.
2
Write 8 IF/THEN rules for it. Example: IF user says "favourite animal" THEN reply "I love robotic dogs!"
3
Include a fallback rule: IF nothing matches THEN reply "Hmm, I'm still learning! Ask me something else?"
4
Have Dad play the role of user while you "run" the chatbot using your rules. Did any conversation break your rules?
💡 In Scratch, you can actually code this chatbot using the "ask and answer" blocks combined with IF/THEN blocks. If you have time, try coding it — the jump from paper to working program is incredibly satisfying for kids!

Chapter 4
Teachable Machine: Image Classifier
Train real AI to recognise YOUR photos
Google made a free tool called Teachable Machine that lets ANYONE train a real AI image classifier — no coding required! You show it examples of things, it learns the patterns, and then it can recognise those things from your camera. This is REAL machine learning — the same type used in professional AI!
🌐 Teachable Machine
Free, in-browser, works on iPad. No account needed. Train image, sound, or pose classifiers in minutes!
Website: teachablemachine.withgoogle.com
How to train an image classifier 📸
1
Choose 2-3 categories (e.g. "thumbs up," "thumbs down," "nothing")
2
Take 30-50 webcam photos of each category — the AI needs examples!
3
Click "Train Model" — watch the AI learn in real time!
4
Test it live! Show your hand — does AI recognise thumbs up vs thumbs down?
🎉
The AI you train on Teachable Machine is a REAL neural network — the exact same technology used in professional AI systems at Google, Apple, and OpenAI! You're not playing with a toy version. You're using the real thing, simplified so kids can access it. That's genuinely awesome.
🎯 Quick Quiz!
Q: Why do you need many photos for each category when training Teachable Machine?
📸 Activity Time!
Train Your Own Image Classifier!
1
Go to teachablemachine.withgoogle.com and click "Get Started" → "Image Project."
2
Create 3 classes: "Marty happy 😄," "Marty surprised 😮," and "Marty silly 🤪"
3
Take 40+ webcam photos for each expression — vary your angle, distance, and lighting!
4
Train it! Test it! Can it tell the difference between your faces? What confuses it?
💡 This is one of the most memorable AI activities for kids — they train a real neural network to recognise their OWN face expressions, then see it working live. Pure magic. Take a video of the live test to share with family!

Chapter 5
Teachable Machine: Sound Classifier
Train AI to recognise sounds with your microphone
Teachable Machine can also learn from SOUNDS! You record examples of different sounds, the AI learns the patterns in the audio waves, and then it can recognise those sounds in real time. This is the same technology behind voice assistants, music recognition apps, and speech-to-text tools!
✅
Fun sounds to classify
  • Clap vs snap vs stomp
  • "Yes" vs "No" vs "Maybe"
  • Whistle vs hum vs sing
  • Background noise vs silence
    • 💡
    Tips for better results
    • Record in a quiet room
    • Vary your volume and speed
    • Record at least 20 samples per class
    • Include a "background noise" class!
    🎵
    SHAZAM — the app that recognises any song in seconds — uses sound AI just like this! It converts audio into a visual "fingerprint" (called a spectrogram) and matches it against millions of song fingerprints. Your sound classifier does the exact same thing, just on a smaller scale. You're building Shazam technology!
    🎯 Quick Quiz!
    Q: Why is it important to include a "background noise" class when training a sound classifier?
    🎙️ Activity Time!
    Build a Clap-Controlled AI!
    1
    In Teachable Machine, start a new Sound project.
    2
    Create 3 classes: "Clap," "Snap," and "Background Noise."
    3
    Record 20+ samples for each. For Background Noise, just record room sounds.
    4
    Train and test! Can your AI tell a clap from a snap? Try fooling it with different types of claps!
    💡 Advanced extension: Teachable Machine lets you export the model and connect it to Scratch! That means you could build a Scratch game controlled by clapping. This is genuine real-world human-computer interaction design.

    Chapter 6
    Make an AI Story Generator
    Use AI as a co-author for wild adventures
    What if you could give AI the start of a story and it would fill in wild adventures? Today you'll become a story DIRECTOR — you set the rules, the characters, and the world. Then AI helps generate what happens next. The result? Stories you could never have written alone!
    The story generator recipe 📖
    Fill in the blanks to create your story prompt!
    🧒 Hero: A 7-year-old called ________ who can ________
    🌍 Setting: A world where ________
    😈 Problem: Suddenly ________
    🤖 Twist: The only way to solve it involves AI that can ________
    ✍️
    Authors like to say that "AI is a brilliant first draft machine." The ideas that come from you — the human — are what make a story special. AI can fill in plot twists and descriptions quickly, but the heart of any story comes from the writer's own experiences, feelings, and imagination. Your creativity is irreplaceable!
    🎯 Quick Quiz!
    Q: When using AI to help write a story, who provides the most important creative ingredient?
    📖 Activity Time!
    Co-Write an AI Adventure Story!
    1
    Fill in the story template above. Marty picks the hero, setting, problem, and AI twist.
    2
    Dad types it into ChatGPT or Claude and asks: "Write a 3-paragraph adventure story using this setup."
    3
    Read the AI's story. Did it understand your idea? What parts surprised you?
    4
    Now YOU write the ending — make it better than what AI came up with! Compare the two endings.
    💡 Marty's ending will almost certainly have more personality and heart than the AI's version. This is the most effective way to help kids understand AI's limitations — through direct creative comparison!

    Chapter 7
    AI Art Gallery — Your Own Prompts
    Become a prompt engineer and curate your gallery
    This week you become a PROMPT ENGINEER — someone who gets the best results from AI by crafting perfect descriptions. The secret? More detail = better art! "A dog" gets a boring result. "A golden retriever astronaut floating in space eating a pink ice cream, in the style of a children's book illustration" gets something magical!
    The anatomy of a great prompt 🎨
    1. SUBJECT
    The main thing: "a robot scientist"
    2. ACTION
    What it's doing: "discovering a glowing plant"
    3. SETTING
    Where: "inside a cosy underground laboratory"
    4. STYLE
    Art style: "in the style of a watercolour children's book"
    5. MOOD/LIGHTING
    Feeling: "warm golden light, magical atmosphere, cosy"
    💰
    "Prompt Engineer" is now a real, well-paying job — companies hire people specifically to get the best results from AI tools! The skill is about understanding how AI interprets language and crafting descriptions that lead to exactly the output you want. It's part art, part science, and part psychology. You're learning it right now!
    🎯 Quick Quiz!
    Q: Which AI art prompt will probably give the best, most specific result?
    🎨 Activity Time!
    Create Your AI Art Gallery!
    1
    Write 5 detailed prompts using the Subject + Action + Setting + Style + Mood formula above.
    2
    Use Adobe Firefly (firefly.adobe.com) — free and kid-safe — to generate each one.
    3
    Print out your favourites or save them to a folder called "Marty's AI Gallery."
    4
    For each image, write a title card: what the prompt was, what you expected vs what you got.
    💡 Print the best images and hang them in Marty's room or on the fridge! Physical output from a digital creative process is very satisfying and gives a real sense of accomplishment. These are genuinely HIS artworks.

    Chapter 8
    Make AI Music
    Compose your own tunes with AI tools
    This chapter is pure fun — you're going to use AI to make actual music! No musical training needed. AI music tools let you compose by picking moods, styles, and instruments, or by describing what you want in words. You're the composer — AI is your orchestra!
    Kid-friendly AI music tools 🎹
    🎵 Chrome Music Lab — Song Maker
    Tap a coloured grid to compose a melody. No AI but a beautiful visual way to understand how melody and rhythm work. The foundation for understanding AI music!
    musiclab.chromeexperiments.com → Song Maker
    🤖 Boomy.com — AI Song Creator
    Pick a genre (lo-fi, electronic, pop...) and AI generates a complete song with melody, bass, and drums. Free to use! You can even customise and download your songs.
    boomy.com — free account, kid-friendly with parent
    🎸
    More than 3 million songs have been created on Boomy — and some have been played on Spotify and Apple Music! AI music is already part of everyday life: background music in YouTube videos, app sound effects, and meditation playlists are often AI-generated. You've probably heard AI music without knowing it!
